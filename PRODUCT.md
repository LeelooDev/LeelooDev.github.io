# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The primary audience is recruiters, engineering managers, and senior engineers evaluating a candidate for Senior iOS Engineer roles, especially teams working in medical imaging, digital health, realtime collaboration, or trustworthy AI systems. They scan quickly, compare evidence against a job description, and need to understand both technical depth and delivery scope without decoding a long personal narrative.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio should earn qualified interviews by presenting a clear professional position: a senior iOS engineer who can own native mobile delivery, understands clinical and DICOM workflows, and can extend products into web, backend, realtime, and local AI systems. Success means a visitor can identify the target role, differentiators, strongest evidence, and relevant project links within a few minutes.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and evidence-driven. The voice should feel like an experienced engineer explaining real systems to another experienced professional: confident without exaggeration, technically specific without keyword stuffing, and attentive to users in high-trust healthcare settings.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic resume templates, inflated claims, unverifiable metrics, AI-generated slogans, tool-name inventories, and flashy corporate landing-page language. Do not present AI-assisted coding tools as a substitute for engineering judgment. Do not bury the target role beneath biography, or make every project sound equally important.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the role and domain: make Senior iOS, medical imaging, and AI systems visible before supporting details.
2. Show evidence instead of adjectives: connect each claim to a product, architecture decision, observable capability, repository, or written case study.
3. Preserve professional focus: use web, backend, and AI work to strengthen the iOS positioning, not dilute it into an undefined generalist profile.
4. Make complex work scannable: prioritize outcomes, responsibilities, and technical decisions in that order.
5. Respect uncertainty: omit private details and unknown metrics rather than inventing precision.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, semantic headings, keyboard-accessible links, responsive layouts, meaningful image alternatives, and support for reduced motion. Chinese and English versions should communicate the same professional position without literal or awkward translation.
